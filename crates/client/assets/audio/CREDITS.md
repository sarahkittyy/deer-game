## Shallow-water step splashes

Six one-shot "step in ankle-deep water" splashes, all cut from one CC0 field recording of someone wading in shallow water
(lifting the feet out and putting them back in), so they share one character and can be picked at random per step.
Freesound HQ preview MP3 (`https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/...`); the sound page states "Creative Commons 0" (checked at download time).
No sip/slurp sound is included: the CC0 candidates found (dog lapping from a pond, horse drinking, cat drinking) were not cleaner
or more suitable than the existing `drink_1/2.ogg`.

Processing (all files): source is dual-mono, mixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz; high-pass 120 Hz (2 x 2-pole) to remove
wind/handling rumble; mild FFT denoise (`afftdn=nr=12:nf=-60:tn=1`); cut as noted; leading quiet trimmed (onset kept with 8 ms
pre-roll), trailing quiet trimmed; 4 ms fade-in, 100 ms fade-out; loudness matched to the existing `step_*.ogg`
(-22.3 LUFS, measured as EBU R128 loudness of the file looped 6x back-to-back because the files are shorter than 400 ms;
step_1/2/3/6 measure -22.6/-22.2/-21.7/-22.6 the same way), peak <= -1 dBFS (splash_1 is peak-limited, -23.6 LUFS);
encoded as Ogg Vorbis quality 4 (libvorbis).
